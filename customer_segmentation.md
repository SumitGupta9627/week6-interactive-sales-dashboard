# Customer Segmentation

Customer segmentation divides customers into groups based on their sales activity.

## Segments

### High Value Customer
Customers with higher total sales.

### Regular Customer
Customers with medium sales activity.

### Low Value Customer
Customers with lower sales activity.

## Purpose

Customer segmentation helps identify valuable customers and understand customer purchasing patterns.

## Data Used

- Customer ID
- Total Sales
- Quantity

## Visualization

An interactive Plotly visualization can be used to compare customers based on their total sales and purchasing activity.
